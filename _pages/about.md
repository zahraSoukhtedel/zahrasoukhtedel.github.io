I am Zahra Soukhtedel, an M.Sc. student in Data Science for Health (DS4Health) at Institut Polytechnique de Paris. I work on deep learning for medical image analysis, and I am particularly interested in vision transformers, foundation models and multimodal learning.

I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Science from Sharif University of Technology, where my thesis, supervised by Dr. Tefagh, was on 3D medical image segmentation: combining the MedSAM foundation model with Neural Radiance Fields (NeRFs) and exploring X-ray-to-CT reconstruction. I was also a remote research assistant at Duke University, working on speech recognition for Persian and on a RAG + Vision Transformer system for question answering over movies and TV series.

Before starting my master's, I spent two and a half years as a Data Scientist on the AI & Big Data team at Digikala, Iran's leading e-commerce platform. There I built production deep-learning systems across search, computer vision and NLP, including a hybrid semantic search system trained with contrastive learning, ViT-based visual search, and an LLM-powered shopping assistant.

## Research Interests
