## **Industry Experience**

### **Data Scientist at Digikala (AI & Big Data Team)**  
*<span style="font-size: smaller; color: gray;"> February 2024 - September 2026 | Tehran, Iran</span>*

Owned and contributed to production deep-learning systems across search, computer vision and NLP at Iran's leading e-commerce platform, from research and model training to deployment.

- **Semantic search & contrastive representation learning (Search team):** Built transformer-based dense retrieval in which language models embed both user queries and product texts in a shared space; trained a query–product text-to-text dual-encoder with a contrastive learning objective and improved it through successive rounds of training experiments. Initiated the idea of a hybrid model and trained it to fuse lexical (Elasticsearch) and semantic results, giving both exact matching and semantic relevance. Results of the hybrid system: ~10% recall gain, **+4.46%** search conversion rate and **+4.5%** search revenue.
- **Visual search with Vision Transformers:** Used ViT image embeddings for image-based product search, and implemented open-vocabulary object detection to localise multiple objects in user-uploaded photos, enabling multi-object image search.
- **Image classification:** Trained a ViT-based colour-classification model (classification head on a ViT backbone) mapping product images to a standardised colour taxonomy.
- **Query understanding:** Fine-tuned a BERT-based classifier to predict product categories from search queries for intent recognition, giving **+2.7%** search conversion rate.
- **LLM & retrieval systems:** Built an AI shopping assistant (RAG, LLM function calling, prompt engineering) and a vector-database FAQ retrieval system that grounds the assistant's answers in site documentation.
- **Privacy-preserving text analysis:** Classified customer feedback and detected issues in NPS comments with in-house LLMs, migrating from external APIs to internal pipelines for data privacy, customisation and scalable labelling.
